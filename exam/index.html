Host It Yourself

Ever wondered how cloud services work or wanted to escape big tech? Let me introduce you to self-hosting. Learn how it works and have your own photo service up and running by today!

My own Google Photos?

Why quit Google Photos?

Your photos are the most personal thing you own and right now they are stored on someone else’s computer. Google Photos is convenient; that’s the whole trick. You’re paying them a monthly fee, while they collect data to build up an advertisement profile. And the rules can change whenever a company decides they should. Your memories shouldn’t work like a subscription.

What is Immich?

Immich is a free and open-source photo service built to replicate the look and feel of Google Photos. It’s mainly focused at providing privacy and sovereignty with the following advantages being central:

  • Free and open-source
  • Runs on anything
  • Your photos never leave your house

What if they lived at home instead?

Introducing self-hosting: running software on a computer you own instead of renting the service from someone else. For photo’s THE app for this is Immich. It’s free and open-source and really well-built. It looks and feels like the gallery apps your used to, as it’s meant to emulate the experience you’d get from Google Photos. It has support for automatic backups, smart search, albums and sharing.

Don't fear the hard part

You don’t need expensive gear, a computer science degree or a rack humming in a basement. A spare PC or laptop and a spare drive are enough to get started. This site takes you the whole way: what Immich is, what it can do, how to install it and how to keep your photos safe. It’s a weekend project with a lifetime payoff.
